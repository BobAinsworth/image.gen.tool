Subject basics:

(1) In a sentence or two, briefly describe the image you wish to create. The following questions will give you a chance to provide a lot more details.

Example: “I'd like a photograph of an older woman standing in a garden, wearing casual clothes holding freshly picked vegetables.”

(2) What is the purpose or point of the image?

Example: “Professional LinkedIn headshot with a friendly, trustworthy feel.”

(3) Who is the intended audience for the image?

(4) What is the age category of the person?

(5) What is the gender presentation?

Face & body:

(6) What is the ethnicity or facial characteristics?

(7) What emotion or expression should the person show?

(8) What facial features should the person have?

(9) Physical-appearance choices

(10) What color hair does the subject have?

(11) What hairstyle does the subject have?

(12) What is the person’s overall stature?

Framing & pose:

(13) What is the camera framing?

(14) What pose or body position should they have?

(15) What clothing style should they wear?

Environment & lighting:

(16) What is the environment or background?

(17) In what direction is the subject looking?

(18) What direction should the lighting come from?

(19) What lighting style should be used?

Style & technical:

(20) What art style or realism level do you want?

(21) What lens style or technical details should apply?

(22) What aspect ratio should the final image use?

Extras & mood:

(23) Any additional elements to include?

(24) Any elements to avoid?

(25) What is the tone or mood of the overall image?

(26) Is there anything else you wish to describe?
